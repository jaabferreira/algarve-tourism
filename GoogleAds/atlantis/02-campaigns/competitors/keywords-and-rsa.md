# Competitors Campaign — Keywords & RSA

**Campaign goal:** capture searches for direct Portimão / Algarve boat-tour competitors and offer Atlantis as the comparison choice. Targets searchers already in market and already considering a specific other operator — high commercial intent, but lower Quality Score (and therefore higher CPC) because our landing page doesn't match the searched brand name.

**Competitors targeted:**
1. Algarve Experience
2. Dreamwave
3. Xride
4. Algarve Discovery
5. Royal Nautic

---

## ⚠️ Google policy reminder (read first)

| Rule | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Bidding on competitor brand names is allowed** | Anywhere except a few jurisdictions. Portugal/EU is fine. |
| **Using competitor trademark in ad text is NOT allowed** | RSA headlines and descriptions must NOT contain "Algarve Experience", "Dreamwave", "Xride", "Algarve Discovery", "Royal Nautic" or close misspellings. Google auto-rejects. |
| **Trademark complaints can shut down keywords** | If a competitor files a trademark complaint with Google, the keyword may be restricted to "near-match only" (typos, related). The campaign keeps running, just with smaller reach. |
| **Quality Score will be low** | 3-5/10 is normal for competitor bidding. CPC runs ~2-3× higher than your own brand. Expected and accepted. |

The RSA below uses no competitor brand names — only generic positioning ("Compare Algarve Boat Tours", "Atlantis Tours — Direct").

| Devices | Mobile **+20%**, Tablet -20%, Desktop default |
| Ad rotation | "Optimize: prefer best-performing ads" |
| Ad schedule | All day, all week |

**Why max CPC €1.50** (higher than C2 Brand's €0.50): low Quality Score on competitor keywords means we need to bid more to win the auction. €1.50 is a defensible ceiling — more would burn cash on irrelevant clicks.

**Why €5/day** (small): competitor bidding is **defensive / opportunistic**, not core volume. Cap exposure until search-terms report shows it's pulling real conversions.

## Within-campaign negative keywords

These are **critical** because some competitor names contain generic Algarve words.

```
[algarve experience day]
[best algarve experience]
[algarve experience guide]
[algarve discovery card]
[algarve discovery pass]
[algarve discovery walks]
[algarve discovery trip]
"is it worth"
review
reviews
"vs"
"versus"
careers
jobs
```

**Why "review" / "vs" / "is it worth":** people searching these are researching the competitor, not in market. They'll click curiously and bounce.

**Why the "algarve discovery card/pass/walks":** Algarve Discovery is the brand name, but "algarve discovery" is also a phrase used for tourism cards, walking tours, and other unrelated products. Block those.

**Why the "algarve experience day/guide":** "algarve experience" can match generic "best algarve experience" queries — block long-tail variants that aren't about the operator.

## Expected KPIs (Phase 1, Competitors)

**Working assumption:** avg booking value €100 (mostly Benagil self-selection from `/tours/` listing — competitor searchers tend to want the comparable cheap product).

| Metric | Target | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Avg CPC | €0.80 – €1.50 | Higher than other campaigns due to low QS on competitor keywords |
| CTR | ≥ 2% | Lower than other campaigns — many will recognize "Atlantis Tours" isn't who they searched and skip |
| Conv rate (click → FH purchase) | ≥ 1% | Comparison shoppers convert lower than direct intent |
| Cost per booking (CPA) | **€30 – €60** | 30-60% of €100 — accepting tighter margin because steal-share strategy |
| ROAS | ≥ 2× | Lower than primary campaigns; this is share-stealing, not optimization |
| Bookings/month (target) | 1 – 5 | Niche; treat any conversion as a win |

⚠️ **Hard floor:** if CPA exceeds **€80** over a 30-day window, pause or shrink keyword list to only the brands actually converting.

⚠️ **Trademark complaint risk:** if a competitor files a complaint with Google, you'll get an email and the affected keyword(s) will be limited to misspellings only. Don't fight it — adjust and continue. The campaign survives.

---

## Maintenance checklist

- **Day 1-7:** check search-terms report **daily**. Quality Score will be ugly (3-5/10) — that's expected. Watch for any unrelated queries triggering and add tight negatives.
- **Week 2-4:** if a specific competitor brand consistently drives 0 conversions while others convert, **pause that competitor's keywords only** — don't kill the whole campaign.
- **Month 2:** evaluate whether to expand the competitor list (add new operators discovered in the auction insights report) or contract (drop ones not pulling weight).
- **Watch the Auction Insights report** — it will show which other operators are bidding on the same keywords. Useful intel for who to add to the list.

---

## Notes

- This is a tactical, low-volume campaign. Don't expect it to drive primary revenue.
- Best case: competitor's customer sees Atlantis ad → recognizes the brand → considers the comparison → books direct.
- Worst case: ad shows but gets ignored. Limited downside thanks to small daily budget.
- If trademark complaints come in for multiple competitors, consider scaling back to bidding only on misspellings/typos which avoid complaints entirely.

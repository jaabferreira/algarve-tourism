# Cranchi Yacht Campaign — Keywords & RSA

**Campaign goal:** capture high-intent searches for "private yacht charter Algarve" / "luxury yacht Portimão" / "private boat to Benagil" — the premium segment. Low volume, high margin per booking.

**Why this campaign exists separate from Benagil:** completely different audience (couples, special occasions, planners — not last-minute walk-ups), completely different price point (€473-€894 vs €20), and different ad copy framing (private/exclusive/luxury vs accessible/daily). Mixing them in one campaign would dilute both.

**Routing:** all clicks land on the Cranchi Yacht product page (PK `720028`, EN slug `cranchi-yacht-cruise-to-the-benagil-caves`).

**Product reality check** (sourced from FareHarbor):

| Variant | PK | Duration | Price from |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard cruise | 720028 | 2h30 | €473.68 |
| Half Day | 720080 | 3h30 | €715.79 |
| Full Day | 720086 | 6h | €894.74 |

All variants: up to 10 guests, private (just your group + captain/crew), departures from Portimão, includes Benagil cave visit. Optional paid add-ons (hydrofoil, jet-ski) — not advertised in copy.

**Why max CPC €2.50** (much higher than Benagil): at avg booking value ~€600 (mid-point of variants) and target CPA 15-20% of revenue = €90-€120, even a 3% conv rate supports €2.70-€3.60 max CPC. €2.50 is a conservative ceiling that lets us win premium auctions.

**Why mobile +10% (vs +30% on Benagil):** premium private-yacht searches skew more toward desktop research sessions. Still positive on mobile, just smaller premium.

## Expected KPIs (Phase 1, Cranchi Yacht)

**Working assumption:** avg booking value ~€600 (mix of 2h30 €473, half-day €716, full-day €895).

| Metric | Target | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Avg CPC | €1.50 – €2.50 | High-intent / high-value queries; competition exists |
| CTR | ≥ 4% | Lower than Benagil — premium intent is more comparison-heavy |
| Conv rate (click → FH purchase) | ≥ 1.5% (target 3%) | High consideration; many users research before booking |
| Cost per booking (CPA) | **€60 – €120** | 10-20% of €600 avg booking value |
| ROAS | ≥ 5× | Premium product margin supports this |
| Bookings/month (target) | 3 – 8 | Low-volume product; double-digit months indicate strong fit |

⚠️ **Hard floor:** if CPA exceeds **€150** (25% of €600) over a 30-day window, pause and reassess. Could be wrong copy, wrong intent matching, or over-bidding.

⚠️ **Watch consideration time:** premium yacht bookings often have a 7-30 day path — user clicks the ad, browses, leaves, comes back days later via direct or organic, then books. Set GA4 attribution lookback to 30 days minimum. Don't judge the campaign on same-session conversions alone.

---

## Maintenance checklist

- **Week 1-2:** daily search-terms review. Premium queries are diverse — expect to add 5-15 negatives per week initially (especially "for sale" / "used" intent slipping through).
- **Week 3:** if you see no conversions yet, **don't pause** — the consideration cycle is longer than Benagil's. Wait until day 21.
- **Month 2:** if conversion volume ≥ 10 in last 30 days, switch bidding to **Maximize Conversion Value**. Important here because the price spread (€473-€895) means optimizing for *value* matters more than *count*.
- **Month 3:** consider adding a remarketing audience layer — visitors who landed on the Cranchi page but didn't book. Phase 2 territory.

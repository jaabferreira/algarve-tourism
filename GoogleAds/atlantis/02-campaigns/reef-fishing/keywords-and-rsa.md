# Reef Fishing Campaign — Keywords & RSA

**Campaign goal:** capture "fishing tour Algarve" / "pesca Portimão" / "boat fishing" intent. Different audience entirely from Benagil (caves sightseers) or the yachts (premium private charters). Buyers are usually **groups of friends**, **fathers with kids**, or **tourists wanting a different kind of Algarve day**.

**Why this campaign exists separate from the others:** fishing intent doesn't overlap with cave tours, sailing, or private yacht charter. Mixing it anywhere else would dilute everyone's Quality Score. It's also lower-CPC territory (less premium-yacht competition), so bidding and budget are different.

**Routing:** all clicks land on the Reef Fishing product page (PK `718024`, slug `reef-fishing-tour` same across locales).

**Product reality check:**

| Group size | Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1-5 pax | **€304.88 flat** | Whole boat for a small group; ≈ €61/pax at 5 pax |
| 6-16 pax | **€84.22 / pax** | Scales from €505 (6 pax) to €1,347 (16 pax) |

Duration 4h, departs Portimão Naval Club (Cais de São Francisco — different dock from Benagil/yacht), anchors at a reef ~5km offshore. Catches include sea bream, snapper, sea bass, gilthead, grouper. **All ages welcome.** Guests can take their catch home.

⚠️ The pricing structure matters for ad copy: "From €84" is technically correct for group-of-6+, but for solo/small-group searchers it's *actually* a €305 flat charter. We lead with "From €84" in ads because it's the lower number, but the landing page clarifies the two pricing tiers.

**Why max CPC €1.00** (between Benagil's €0.80 and Cranchi's €2.50): avg booking ~€400 supports a CPA of €60-80 at 15-20%. At 3% conv rate that allows up to €1.80-€2.40 CPC — €1.00 is a conservative starting ceiling. Fishing CPCs typically run lower than yacht anyway because competition is thinner.

**Why +30% geo** (matches Benagil, not the yachts): fishing bookings skew toward last-minute "what can we do today" decisions more than yacht charters, which are planned.

## Expected KPIs (Phase 1, Reef Fishing)

**Working assumption:** avg booking value ~€400 (mix of €305 small-group flat, larger groups €500-800). Some big group bookings can spike this much higher.

| Metric | Target | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Avg CPC | €0.50 – €1.00 | Thin competition; should run under ceiling |
| CTR | ≥ 5% | Niche intent + clear headline should hold |
| Conv rate (click → FH purchase) | ≥ 2% (target 4%) | Higher than yacht because decision cycle is shorter |
| Cost per booking (CPA) | **€40 – €80** | 10-20% of €400 avg booking value |
| ROAS | ≥ 5× | Niche product with strong margin |
| Bookings/month (target) | 3 – 10 | Fishing is niche but bookings are high-value per unit |

⚠️ **Hard floor:** if CPA exceeds **€100** (25% of €400) over a 30-day window, pause and tighten.

⚠️ **Watch for group-size skew:** if the campaign attracts mostly small-group (1-5) bookings, revenue per booking drops to €305, and CPA targets need to drop to €45-€60 to stay within margin. Monitor avg booking value in GA4 weekly.

## Maintenance checklist

- **Week 1-2:** daily search-terms review. Fishing-related queries are noisy — expect to add 10-15 negatives per week (license / rod / tournament / big-game).
- **Week 3:** if avg booking skews heavily toward small-group €305 flat, consider cutting ceiling CPC to €0.70.
- **Month 2:** if volume ≥ 30 in last 30 days, switch to **Maximize Conversion Value** (important given the wide price spread €305-€1,347).
- **Month 3:** consider adding a dedicated "family fishing" ad group — family-intent queries convert at higher rates than generic fishing queries.

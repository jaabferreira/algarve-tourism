# Atlantis Tours — competitor SERP teardown

*2026-05-12 · companion to `SEO/research/2026-05-12-atlantis-keyword-map.md` and `SEO/audits/2026-05-12-atlantis-organic-diagnosis.md`. Focus angles: SERP overlap, content depth, brand/entity strength, and a qualitative read on the backlink gap. No Ahrefs/Semrush/Similarweb — backlink and traffic figures here are inferred, not measured; flagged where that matters. SERP snapshots were taken via a US-localized web search, so the **actual PT / UK / DE / FR SERPs Atlantis competes in will differ in ordering** — the *cast of competitors* is reliable, the exact ranks are directional.*

---

## 1. Five findings

1. **The real organic competitors are not the paid competitors.** The Google Ads "Competitors" campaign bids against Algarve Experience, Dreamwave, Xride, Algarve Discovery, Royal Nautic — but most of those are **Albufeira / Vilamoura** operators that barely show up in the Portimão/Benagil organic results. The domains that actually own page 1 for our money keywords fall into two groups: **(a) OTA aggregators** — GetYourGuide (incl. its per-supplier pages), Viator, Tiqets, Headout, TUI Musement, Civitatis; for fishing, FishingBooker and Tom's Catch — and **(b) descriptive / near-exact-match-domain operator sites** — `carvoeirocaves.com`, `benagilexpress.com`, `5emotionsalgarve.com`, `algarvecavetours.com`, `birdmi.com`. AlgarExperience (`algarexperience.com`) is the one named competitor that *is* a genuine all-round organic threat.

2. **We can't out-authority the OTAs — so beat them where OTAs are weak.** GetYourGuide/Viator/Tiqets have enormous domain authority and will keep the top transactional slots for "benagil cave tour" for the foreseeable future. But their pages are templated, thin on genuine local knowledge, and not really built for **informational long-tails** ("how to get to benagil cave", "can you swim in benagil cave", "benagil vs marinha", "best time to see dolphins in algarve"), for **"book direct" / operator-specific** intent, or for **freshness and depth**. That space is open — and it's exactly the content-hub clusters (C7–C13 in the keyword map). A "complete guide" content piece already ranks in the fishing SERP (`fishingbooker.com/blog/algarve-fishing/` — "…Complete Guide for 2026"), proof that the format wins even against the aggregators.

3. **The head-to-head competitors are the descriptive-domain operators — and they're beatable on content.** `carvoeirocaves.com` and `benagilexpress.com` each run ~1,500-word homepages with a FAQ block, embedded TripAdvisor reviews + Travelers' Choice / Clean&Safe badges, and **3 languages incl. German** — but **no blog and no educational content at all**. They rank on **exact-match domain + review volume + site age**, not content depth. Atlantis already out-publishes them (13 blog posts; tour pages with ~500–600 words of unique copy, a 19-stop itinerary, reviews, related tours). What Atlantis is missing versus them isn't content quality — it's **domain age, review count, referring domains, and German**.

4. **Every serious competitor here serves German. Atlantis does not.** `carvoeirocaves.com` → PT/ES/DE/EN · `benagilexpress.com` → EN/PT/DE · `algarexperience.com` → EN/PT/ES/FR/DE. This is now the **third independent signal** that the German market is real and that not serving it is costing bookings — alongside `bootstour portimao` pulling 1,639 GSC impressions/90d at avg pos 4.8 (the largest single non-brand query) and the Ads team having built a German ad group.

5. **The gap is authority + entity strength + a missing language — not on-page quality.** Same conclusion the organic diagnosis reached from the inside, now confirmed from the competitor side: Atlantis's pages are already competitive with (often better than) the operator competitors; it's a six-week-old domain with near-zero referring domains and a thin review profile competing against operators with years of TripAdvisor history, directory listings, and partner links. On-page polish helps at the margin; the levers that move the needle are off-page authority, review generation, and (for Germany) a new locale.

## 4. Content gap (topics competitors cover / would cover that Atlantis doesn't own yet)

- **German-language everything.** Not a topic gap — a *language* gap. Every operator competitor has it; Atlantis has the largest measured German demand of any non-brand query and no `de` locale. Highest-impact gap on this list.
- **A "things to do in Portimão" / local hub** — AlgarExperience-style local-authority content; nobody small owns it well; it'd distribute internal authority to all four products. (C13.)
- **"Book direct vs OTA" positioning** — the OTAs win the transactional SERP; an explicit "why book direct with the operator" angle (lower price, no booking fee, you talk to the skipper) is content the aggregators *can't* write. Worth a page/section and FAQ entries.
- **Depth + freshness on the informational pillar** — competitors' "guides" are shallow (carvoeirocaves/benagilexpress have *none*; FishingBooker's is the bar to beat in fishing). Atlantis's 13 posts are the raw material; turning them into a proper pillar-and-cluster with FAQ/Breadcrumb schema and "in 2026" freshening is the move. (C7 + cluster.)
- **Review/UGC volume** — not content the team writes, but the single biggest trust gap vs `carvoeirocaves` / `benagilexpress` (years of TripAdvisor). Ties into the `seo-offpage` work and the open `project_missing_reviews` item (3 tours still have zero reviews).

## 5. Backlink & entity gap (qualitative — no tool)

- **Backlinks:** a domain rebuilt ~April 2026 has near-zero referring domains. The operator competitors have years of accumulated TripAdvisor/Viator/GetYourGuide supplier-profile links, local-directory listings, "things to do in the Algarve" roundup mentions, and hotel/villa partner links. This is the gap the `seo-offpage` run is for. Without closing it, C1 ("benagil cave tour") does not move regardless of page quality.
- **Entity strength:** Atlantis ranks #1–2 on its *own* brand, so the brand entity is fine at small scale — but it has no knowledge panel, no Wikipedia-class citations, and a thin third-party mention footprint. AlgarExperience and the established operators have award-program affiliations ("Discover Portugal", Travelers' Choice) that double as both trust signals and link sources. Getting Atlantis into the equivalent regional award/directory programs is a cheap entity + link win.
- **Reviews:** `project_missing_reviews` — Cranchi yacht, reef fishing, and Benagil+Alvor tours have no reviews shown, which suppresses both rich snippets and conversion versus competitors who display dozens.

## 6. Prioritized gap-closing roadmap

This dovetails with the keyword map's production order — same plan, viewed through the competitor lens.

1. **Beat the OTAs where they're weak — build the content hub.** Pillar (C7) + clusters (C8 caves comparison, C9 dolphins, C10 best time, C11 packing, a "Benagil cave rules / can you swim" piece), all with FAQ + BreadcrumbList schema, all interlinked and funnelling to C1/C3. This is the only content lever that out-ranks aggregators. → `pillar-content-architecture` + `content-brief-authoring`.
2. **Close the operator-competitor gaps — off-page + reviews.** Get listed in the same directories/award programs as carvoeirocaves/benagilexpress/AlgarExperience; claim & link operator profiles on TripAdvisor / GetYourGuide / Viator / Civitatis; partner links from hotels/villas; chase reviews on the 3 review-less tours. → `seo-offpage` + the `project_missing_reviews` follow-up.
3. **Enter the biggest market — a German locale.** Every competitor has it; it's the largest non-brand demand; it's a real build (new `de` in i18n + FH locale map + hreflang + sitemap). Scope separately, but it's the highest-ceiling structural move.
4. **Win the "almost there" SERPs now.** C9 dolphins (~pos 7–12) and C2 multilingual CTR (already pos 4–8, terrible CTR) — modest on-page work, no authority needed, fastest visible wins.
5. **Out-position the OTAs on intent.** A "book direct with the operator" page + FAQ entries; explicit pricing/no-fee/talk-to-the-skipper messaging the aggregators structurally can't match.
6. **Don't chase the paid competitors organically.** Dreamwave/Xride/Royal Nautic/Algarve Discovery are Albufeira/Vilamoura operators with little Portimão/Benagil organic overlap — leave their brand terms to the paid "Competitors" campaign; they're not the organic battle.

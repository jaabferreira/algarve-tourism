01Executive Summary

Three things to remember.

The opportunity, the bet, and the discipline that makes it pay back.

i.

The customer is already on the beach.

Most bookings come from English-speaking tourists who are physically in Portugal and book the same day or the day before. We win or lose this audience on a phone, in the next 30 minutes.

ii.

OTAs are taxing what we should own.

Every booking that goes through GetYourGuide or Viator costs us 20%+ in commission. A small slice of pre-trip search ads in the UK, Ireland, and Germany can intercept those bookings before the marketplace does.

iii.

Discipline before scale.

No spend goes live until conversion tracking is verified. We start small, prove ROAS, then let Google's automation amplify what works. Phase the budget — don't pour money into a leaky bucket.

02The Approach

Walk, then run.

Search-only first to learn. Search + Performance Max once the algorithm has earned its training data.

Phase I
May → mid-JuneThe Listening Season
  • Pure Search. Four campaigns: Brand, Benagil, Yacht & Fishing, Pre-trip Planners.
  • Manual CPC bidding. Tight control while we calibrate.
  • 50% of budget. Soft launch — gather conversions, prove the funnel.
  • Daily reviews the first two weeks; weekly thereafter.
Phase II
Mid-June → SeptemberThe Scaling Season
  • Smart Bidding. Switch to Maximize Conversion Value with target ROAS.

  • Customer Match seeds — past FareHarbor customers fuel lookalikes.

07Known Risks & Guardrails

What could break it.

The most likely failure modes — and what we've built into the plan to catch them.

High

Conversion tracking silently breaks

Smart Bidding optimises against bad data. Mitigation: weekly tracking-health check; pause automation if >5% of bookings arrive without a value.

Medium

OTAs bid on our brand keyword

Steal traffic we already won. Mitigation: Brand campaign with €2/day floor and exact match — they can't dislodge us cheaply.

Medium

Budget burns out before peak

Spending €30/day in early May leaves nothing for July. Mitigation: per-campaign daily caps and account-level max-spend backstop.

Medium

Performance Max cannibalises Search

Notorious in Phase II. Mitigation: brand exclusions on PMax + weekly Search CTR check; reduce PMax if Search drops >20%.

Low

Click fraud / bot traffic

Boat tour space attracts some affiliate scrapers. Mitigation: Google's invalid-click filter (default on); evaluate ClickCease only if patterns warrant in Phase II.

Cert.

Off-season ROAS collapses

Algarve demand is seasonal. Mitigation: staged budget reduction — 25% Oct, 10% Nov, full pause Dec–Feb except Brand. Reassess in March.
